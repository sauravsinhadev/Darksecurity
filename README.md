## ✨ Core Features

### 🚨 Anti-Raid & Anti-Nuke Protection
Proactively detects and blocks destructive administrative actions and coordinated member surges in real time.
* **Nuke Prevention:** Monitors mass channel deletions, role wipes, webhook spam, and unauthorized permission changes, stripping permissions or banning compromised staff accounts instantly.
* **Raid Defense:** Identifies suspicious join waves and automated bot accounts, automatically triggering lockdowns or quarantine protocols to keep your community safe.

### 🔐 Advanced Verification & Access Control
Keep bots, raiders, and malicious alt accounts out of your server before they can interact with your members.
* **Customizable Gatekeeping:** Configure multi-step or one-click verification gates tailored to your server's security needs.
* **Role-Based Access:** Automatically assign verified roles upon completion while keeping unverified users isolated from public channels.

### 🤖 Intelligent Auto-Moderation
Maintain a clean, welcoming environment 24/7 without manual micromanagement.
* **Real-Time Filtering:** Automatically detects and removes spam bursts, phishing/malicious links, invite links, and mass-mention abuse.
* **Behavioral Pattern Detection:** Flags suspicious user activity early and applies configurable automated punishments (delete, warn, timeout, or kick).

### 📋 Smart Audit & Event Logging
Maintain full visibility over everything happening in your server with organized, easy-to-read log channels.
* **Comprehensive Tracking:** Logs deleted and edited messages, moderation actions, role updates, channel modifications, and member join/leave events.
* **Clear Accountability:** Includes timestamps, user IDs, and moderator attribution so your staff team always has an accurate audit trail.

### 🔨 Powerful Moderation Suite
Equip your staff with fast, reliable tools to manage day-to-day community operations effortlessly.
* **Full Action Toolkit:** Execute bans, temporary bans, kicks, timeouts, mutes, and structured warning systems with clean embed feedback.
* **Streamlined Workflows:** Built for speed and clarity so moderators can resolve issues in seconds.

### ⚡ Real-Time Security Alerts
Never be caught off guard by an ongoing attack or unusual server behavior.
* **Instant Notifications:** Sends immediate alerts to designated staff channels whenever high-risk thresholds are triggered or suspicious patterns emerge.
